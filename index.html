We guarantee a 60-minute response time for emergency calls in Austin and surrounding areas — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Our technicians are stationed throughout the city so we can get to you fast no matter where you are.

Standard customers pay a small after-hours fee for weekend and holiday calls. However, Premium Maintenance Plan members get FREE weekend and holiday emergency service — no extra charges, ever. It's one of the biggest perks of the plan.
